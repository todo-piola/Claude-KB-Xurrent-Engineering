# Check Availability of Team Members

Last week, the [presence indicator](https://www.xurrent.com/blog/presence-visibility-extended) became visible in more places within the Xurrent Specialist Interface.  This makes it possible to see who is currently active in Xurrent. But when assistance is needed from a specific team, it could be helpful to also see who is currently out of office, as well as who will soon be out of office.

That is why the out-of-office indicator is now displayed in the team records and team previews for the team members who are, or will soon be, unavailable.

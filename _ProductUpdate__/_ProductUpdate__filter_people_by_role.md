# Filter People by Role

A very helpful filter has become available for Xurrent administrators.  This new filter allows them to quickly look up all people who have one or more specific [roles](https://help.xurrent.com/help/roles/) of their organization’s Xurrent account. This new filter is available in all people views and is simply called ‘Role’.

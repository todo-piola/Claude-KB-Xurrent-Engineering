# Dashboards Created By Me

Account administrators and account designers now have an extra view in the ‘[Dashboards](https://www.xurrent.com/product-updates/build-your-own-dashboards)‘ section of the settings console.  This new view is called ‘Dashboards Created By Me’.

This additional view makes it easier for them to distinguish between the dashboards they prepared and the shared dashboards that were created by others.

# More Knowledge Article Filters

The knowledge article views in the Records console can now be filtered by several extra filters. These include the four [visibility options](https://www.xurrent.com/product-updates/new-status-and-visibility-options-for-knowledge-articles) that were recently added (i.e. Internal specialists, Covered specialists, Key contacts and End users) as well as the [specific service instances](https://www.xurrent.com/product-updates/knowledge-articles-for-specific-service-instances) that knowledge articles may have been written for.

And because of the emphasis that has been placed on the [Created by, Created and Updated fields of the knowledge articles](https://www.xurrent.com/product-updates/more-knowledge-article-information), these fields are now also available as filters.

# AI Note Assist

With the latest updates to notes in Xurrent, **we’re excited to introduce Note Assist,**our new AI-powered assistant for drafting notes.

This feature was one of the most requested by customers, many of whom were turning to tools outside Xurrent for AI-generated feedback. Now, that support is built right into your workflow.

**How to use this feature:**

When you start drafting a message, you’ll see the Note Assist “wand” icon appear in the text box. Click it for **immediate suggestions** on how to improve your message. Changes can be applied with a single click, all while keeping full control over the final version.

The Note Assist feature aligns with **Xurrent’s AI philosophy: specialists stay in the driver’s seat, with AI offering guidance, not decisions**. Our goal is to help you work faster and smarter …without compromising your judgment or voice.

If you haven’t already, head over to an Inbox request and give it a try!

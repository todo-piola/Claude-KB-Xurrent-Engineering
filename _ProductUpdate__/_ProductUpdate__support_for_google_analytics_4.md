# Support for Google Analytics 4

In the fall of 2020, Google Analytics 4 (GA4) was rolled out, which replaced Universal Analytics (UA) as the default for digital analytics measurement in Google Analytics.  This also meant that the old UA Tracking ID (example: `UA-196614479-1`) was replaced by the new GA4 Measurement ID, (example: `G-W6BWLVMHJ`).

Google still makes UA Tracking IDs available, but [Xurrent’s Google Analytics app](https://www.xurrent.com/blog/track-self-service-usage-with-google-analytics), which can be found in the ‘Apps’ section of the Settings console, now accepts both ID formats.

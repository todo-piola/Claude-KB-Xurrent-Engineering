# Account Switcher Filter

It is common for certain users in [enterprise environments](https://www.xurrent.com/solutions/enterprise-service-management) to have roles in multiple [support domain accounts](https://www.xurrent.com/product-updates/create-support-domain-accounts). It is also common for Xurrent users of partner organizations and Managed Service Providers which help maintain the Xurrent environments of their customers to also have roles in those customer accounts. People who have roles in many accounts sometimes struggle a bit to quickly find the account they need in the account switcher. For those users, this is now a thing of the past.

When a user has a role in more than 10 accounts, a filter becomes available at the top of the account switcher. Typing a few characters within that filter results in a filtered list of accounts, as can be seen in the screenshot below.

# Xurrent IMR - Q4 2025 Product Updates

#### Compliance Made Simple (and Clickable)

We wrapped up the year by making one of the most tedious parts of the job—audits—a whole lot easier. Whether you are chasing SOC2 compliance or just doing a security review, proving "who changed what and when" usually involves digging through pages of logs or taking messy screenshots.

We’ve introduced Downloadable Audit Logs to solve this. With just one click, you can now export your entire activity history directly from the dashboard. It gives you a clean, organized report perfect for compliance officers or internal analysis.

Navigate to Account → Audit Logs → Download Report to get started.

‍

#### Clean Up Your War Room Chat

If you use Microsoft Teams for incident response, you know the pain of "Channel Clutter." Previously, every single time an incident updated—whether the status changed or a severity level increased—Xurrent would post a brand new card in the channel. During a fast-moving outage, this meant your chat history became a wall of duplicate notifications, forcing you to scroll up endlessly to find the actual conversation.

We fixed this with Update-in-Place for Microsoft Teams Cards. You now have the option to update the _existing_ incident card with the latest status instead of generating a new one. This keeps your channel clean, ensures the most relevant info is always visible at a glance, and stops the notification spam. You can enable this right now in your Account Customizations.

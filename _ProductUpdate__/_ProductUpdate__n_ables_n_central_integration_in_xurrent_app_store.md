# N-able's N-central Integration in Xurrent App Store

Here at Xurrent, we consider it our mission to make the world more productive, each and every day. Today, we do this by announcing another powerful integration that was added to an already impressive list of strategic connections: N-able N-central. N-able, as the name suggests, enables businesses to monitor, manage, and secure IT environments efficiently. N-central is N-able’s RMM (Remote Monitoring and Management) platform designed for Managed Service Providers (MSPs) and IT teams to manage, monitor, and secure IT environments at scale. The N-able N-central app is now available in the Xurrent App Store.

#### **Key Features of N-central**

✅ **Remote Monitoring & Management (RMM)** – Centralized management of devices, networks, and endpoints.
✅ **Automation & Scripting** – Automates repetitive tasks like software updates, patches, and system checks.
✅ **Patch Management** – Automatically deploys security updates to Windows, macOS, and third-party apps.
✅ **Endpoint Security** – Includes antivirus, malware protection, and monitoring tools.
✅ **Backup & Disaster Recovery** – Ensures data protection with cloud and local backups.
✅ **Network Monitoring** – Tracks uptime, performance, and network health.
✅ **Multi-Tenant Management** – Ideal for MSPs managing multiple clients from a single dashboard.
✅ **Reporting & Compliance** – Generates reports for compliance audits and system performance insights.

Installing the app is easy, especially after reading the knowledge article ‘Installing and Configuring the N-able App’, which is available to all covered specialists. The integration enables organizations to establish rules within their existing N-able Central account to automatically create and manage tickets in Xurrent based on key system events or triggers.

With this app, you can help eliminate manual processes and gain better visibility into your IT environment, all while leveraging Xurrent’s service management capabilities.

Whether it’s responding to a critical system alert, managing device health, or tracking maintenance tasks, this integration ensures that your IT team stays efficient and responsive.

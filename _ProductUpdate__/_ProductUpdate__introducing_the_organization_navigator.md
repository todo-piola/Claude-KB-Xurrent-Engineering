# Introducing the Organization Navigator

The ‘Organization Navigator’ section has been added to the Analytics console.  This new section makes it possible for people using the Xurrent Specialist Interface to get an overview of their organization’s structure.

The navigator can also be opened from the Actions menu after an organization record has been opened.

This navigator is especially helpful for administrators when they want to verify that the parent/child relations between the different organizations have been registered correctly.  They can even see which organizations have their own Xurrent account.  For those organizations the ID of their Xurrent account (which is also the subdomain of the account’s URL) is presented in a green box.

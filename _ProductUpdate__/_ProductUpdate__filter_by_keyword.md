# Filter by Keyword

The filter ‘Keywords’ can now be applied to the views and reports of request templates, knowledge articles and services. This makes it possible to exclude all the records that do not include a specific word in their Keywords field.

Or, like in the screenshot below, to find the ones for which a specific keyword has not yet been specified.

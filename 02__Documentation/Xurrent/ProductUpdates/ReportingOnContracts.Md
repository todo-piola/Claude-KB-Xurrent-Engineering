# Reporting on Contracts

Four new reports have become available for generating visual overviews of the contracts that are registered in Xurrent. [Several contract reports already existed](https://www.xurrent.com/product-updates/reports-for-managing-contracts-and-agreements), but now the following reports have been added:

- Contracts by Category
- Contracts by Customer
- Contracts by Customer Representative
- Contracts by Supplier

In addition, the ‘Customer representative’ filter has been added to the ‘Contracts’ view of the Records console and all the contract reports in the ‘Reports’ section of the Analytics console.

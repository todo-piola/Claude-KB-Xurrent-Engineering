# Five More Knowledge Management Reports

A few basic [knowledge management](https://www.xurrent.com/features/knowledge-management) reports have been added to help organizations manage their knowledge articles.  The five new reports are:

- Knowledge Articles
- Knowledge Articles by Service
- Knowledge Articles by Status
- Registered Knowledge Articles
- Registered Knowledge Articles by Status

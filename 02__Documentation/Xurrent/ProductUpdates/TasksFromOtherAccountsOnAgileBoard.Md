# Tasks From Other Accounts on Agile Board

Within an enterprise organization, it is fairly common to assign tasks to people from other departments. With Xurrent, it is even possible to assign tasks to people of external customer or provider accounts. Organizations can use several different trust settings to manage this. When an implementation task or a risk & impact task is assigned to a team in an organization that use agile boards to organize their workload, it makes sense to be able to add these tasks to one of their agile boards. So far, it had only been possible to add a task from your own support domain account to an agile board of that same support domain account, but this restriction has now been removed. This means, that cooperating with other departments or even other companies has again been made a little bit easier for people working with Xurrent.

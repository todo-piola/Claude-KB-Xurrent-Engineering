# Filter Teams by Coordinator and Manager

Last week the ‘Coordinator’ and ‘Manager’ columns were [added to the team views](https://www.xurrent.com/product-updates/more-team-views). This week, the filters ‘Coordinator’ and ‘Manager’ have become available in these same views.

For organizations with many teams, this can make it easier when, for example, all teams managed by a specific person require an update.

# Managing Section on Person Record

A person record in Xurrent contains basic information about that person, which is always visible, such as their local time and their manager. It also contains detailed information, which is organized in sections that can be opened or closed, such as ‘Contact Details’ and ‘Roles & Teams’. For a person who is a manager of at least one other person, a new section is now shown: ‘Managing’. When opening this section, everyone who reports to this person can be seen listed.

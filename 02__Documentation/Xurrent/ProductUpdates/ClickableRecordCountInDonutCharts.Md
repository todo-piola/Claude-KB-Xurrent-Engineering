# Clickable Record Count in Donut Charts

Reports in Xurrent can sometimes be [displayed in several different ways](https://www.xurrent.com/product-updates/display-report-as-bar-pie-or-donut-chart), one of them being a Donut chart.  Just as a Pie chart, it displays data as a circle where each segment represents a part of the data.  A Donut chart, in addition, also shows the total number of records that is included in the report, displayed in the center or hole of the donut.  The number in the center of the Donut chart is now clickable.  Pressing it will show the underlying data.

The data that is presented after clicking the number in the center of the chart is the same data the user would see after pressing the Actions button and then selecting ‘Show Underlying Data’.

# Filter Service Offerings and Service Instances by SLA

The filter ‘Service level agreement’ has become available for the [service instance](https://help.xurrent.com/help/service_instance/) and [service offering](https://help.xurrent.com/help/service_offering/) views.  This makes it possible to quickly look up the service offering or service instance of a specific SLA.

The real reason for adding this filter, though, is to provide the ability to generate a list of service offerings or service instances that are not related to an SLA.  Such a filtered view makes it possible to find the service offerings that are not used, or to look up the service instances for which a customer still needs to be identified.

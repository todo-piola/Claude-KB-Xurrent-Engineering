# Filter Knowledge Articles by Knowledge Manager

The ‘Knowledge manager’ filter has been added to the knowledge article views and reports.  This new filter makes it possible to limit these views and reports to knowledge articles that are related to a service for which one of the selected people in the filter is the knowledge manager.
